# FocusED educator review and optional classroom pilot

**A free, low-friction way for a teacher to assess FocusED learning resources.** This is a practical invitation, not a formal partnership, research study or claim of educational effectiveness.

FocusED is an early-stage student-built education platform. Its public resources are original and independently developed. They are not official exam-board materials or scored examination submissions.

## Option A: 10-minute teacher-only review

1. **2 minutes — Open and orient.** Choose one resource on your own device. No account creation or learner access is needed for the public resources.
2. **4 minutes — Audit the explanation.** Check question accuracy, marking, curriculum fit and accessibility.
3. **2 minutes — Check independent practice.** Could a student attempt the task before seeing its worked answer?
4. **2 minutes — Give candid feedback.** Identify one strength, one defect and the single improvement that would most help your students.

### Three-question feedback rubric

Rate each from 1 (needs substantial improvement) to 5 (ready for further teacher testing):

- **Accuracy:** Are the content and worked solutions correct?
- **Clarity:** Can an appropriate learner follow the steps without constant teacher intervention?
- **Classroom fit:** Could this be useful as an optional supplementary practice task?

Please report factual errors with the relevant passage or question, not personal student information.

## Option B: An optional small-group pilot after teacher approval

Only consider a pilot **after** the responsible school or learning center and required parents/guardians have approved participation.

- Suggested duration: one or two weeks; one topic or one short worksheet.
- Instructed and supervised by the center's own teacher.
- Voluntary and free; do not replace the existing curriculum or official examinations.
- Participation, accounts and consent must follow the institution's rules and local requirements.
- Begin with the public no-account resources where possible.
- Collect **aggregated, non-identifying feedback**, not names, phone numbers, IDs, exam credentials, or private submissions sent by email.
- Stop the trial if safety, curriculum accuracy, accessibility or data-handling concerns arise.

### Report back

A short report can contain: class or cohort **age band** (not names); which resource was tested; approximately how many students participated (only when reporting is permitted); number completing the exercise; common confusing steps; corrections requested; teacher's decision whether further testing is worthwhile. Do **not** claim the pilot proves better grades or learning gains without an appropriate independent evaluation design.
